# Findings: write probes, part 3: exports, move, merge and recycle-bin restore

**Draft, sitting A only.** Sitting A (local, 2026-11-16 UTC) is in: `write-probes-r2-local`, `write-probes-r2-protected-local` and `write-probes-r2-timeout`. Sitting B (cloud, the label scenario, and sitting A's leftovers) hasn't run. Every cell that waits on it says **pending sitting B**: the cloud (C) halves, LB, O5, RP, D's move and the L↔C steps (Q1). Nothing here goes into the spine memlog until the doc is final.

**Top finding: three calls end ONENOTE.EXE, each reproduced.** `Publish(pfOneNote2007)` of a page (twice), `MergeFiles` (three times, once with File > Options open), and `UpdateHierarchy` moving a page out of a **locked** protected section (once). The first two return `0x800706BE` RPC_S_CALL_FAILED after 1.7–3.0 s; the locked move left no row at all (Crashes). After one crash OneNote didn't relaunch, and on the next two starts it showed its safe-start prompt (observed by Phill). So no tool may send any of the three: entry 2 has to make them refusals before COM, or drop the tool.

**Short answer:**

1. **Exports.** Page and section publish in PDF, XPS, Word, MHTML, HTML, EMF and OneNotePackage, each writing exactly one file; a notebook publishes only as a package. Packages land about 255 ms after the call returns; the others are on disk at the first poll. Word is the slow one (1.0–3.9 s).
2. **A timed-out export isn't a broken one.** The 30-page Word export passed its 1 s limit, its file was on disk 1 ms later at full size and never grew, and a retry to the same path is `0x8004201A` hrFileAlreadyExists, leaving the file untouched.
3. **A notebook package keeps protected sections encrypted**, locked or unlocked (XP1, XP2). That is the opposite of a section's `pfOneNote` backup, which is plaintext (5.1 constraint 17).
4. **`OpenPackage` unpacks but doesn't open.** It wrote the notebook's files straight into the folder it was given and opened no notebook; the probe's own `OpenHierarchy` opened it, and its page appeared within 5 s.
5. **`MergeSections` is unreliable on plain sections.** S1 (within MCP Test) returned `0x8004201F` hrMergeFailed **after copying two of the three pages**, dropping the subpage; S2 (to the throwaway) copied nothing. Three other plain or unlocked merges worked. It copies (the source is untouched) and the copies get new IDs. A locked source or destination is `0x8004201B`.
6. **Moves work, with surprises.** A page moves by one call with the target section's XML (shape a), gets a new ID and keeps its dates, within and across notebooks; leaving it out of the source's XML (shape b) changes nothing. Its **subpage doesn't follow**: it stays behind, promoted to level 1. Sections and section groups keep their IDs, across notebooks too. One cross-notebook section move raised a "hasn't finished downloading" dialog, then returned `0x80042004` **and left a copy** in the target.
7. **Restore works through `UpdateHierarchy`.** A recycled page listed in any section's XML (same section, another section, another notebook) comes back with a new ID and leaves Deleted Pages; a recycled section listed in its group's XML comes back with its ID. OneNote never returned `0x8004202E`: renaming a recycled section, moving a live section into `OneNote_RecycleBin` (a soft delete without `DeleteHierarchy`) and reordering Deleted Pages all succeeded. `UpdatePageContent` on a recycled page is `0x8004200C` hrPageReadOnly.
8. **A modal dialog blocks none of the four calls tried.** `GetHierarchy`, `Publish(pfPDF)`, `OpenPackage` and `MergeSections` all worked with File > Options open; `MergeFiles` crashed there too. D's move is pending sitting B.
9. **Protected content can leave protection.** With the section unlocked, merges and page moves carry pages from a protected section into a plain one, where they sit unencrypted (SP, MP).

**How it ran.** Opt-in scenarios in `tests/OneNoteMcp.Integration/WriteProbes/` (`Round2ExportProbes.cs`, `Round2MergeProbes.cs`, `Round2MoveProbes.cs`, `Round2RestoreProbes.cs`, `Round2ProtectedProbes.cs`, `Round2TimeoutProbe.cs`), run on 2026-11-16 (UTC) by the main session with Phill present, on MCP Test (local), `MCP Test Throwaway 1112-034716 a` (adopted by exact name, Q3) and a throwaway the protected scenario made, `MCP Test Throwaway 1116-025556 p`. Every write went through `TestNotebook`'s guard, and every shown check came back empty. Each row below cites a probe name and a run stamp: search for it in the JSONL logs. Times are UTC. "Observed by Phill" marks what Phill saw in OneNote and reported; it isn't in the logs.

## Implications for entry 2 (3.2 spine amendments) and the round-2 stories

Proposals for Phill, not decisions. Each cites the rows it rests on.

1. **`publish` never offers `pfOneNote2007`** (entry 13's format list, entry 3's tracer). It crashed OneNote both times it was sent (`x1-local-page-pfOneNote2007`, `-023939`, `-024627`). The format enum leaves it out, and the gateway refuses it before COM in case a caller passes the raw value.
2. **`MergeFiles` crashes OneNote every time,** so entry 9's `merge_files` can't ship as planned (`f1-local`, `-024202`, `-024714`; `d-local-merge-files`, `-024952`). Options: (a) drop entry 9, with the Decision closing E4, and entry 10 goes after entry 8; (b) keep the tool name, returning `NotSupported` before COM. Even a target on disk doesn't mean it worked: D's crash left a 10,104-byte file.
3. **Page moves refuse a locked source or destination before any COM call** (entries 4 and 12). A page moved out of a locked section crashed OneNote (`local-locked`, `-025556`). A move into a locked section wasn't tried, but a merge into one returned `0x8004201B` (`sp-local-locked-destination`), and refusing costs nothing. The same goes for moving a locked section until a probe shows it's safe (not probed; sitting B skips it).
4. **`merge_sections` (entry 6) needs a recheck of when it works.** hrMergeFailed came back on both of the Matrix's plain merges, S1 within a notebook and S2 across notebooks (`s1-local`, `s2-local`, `-024952`), while S3, D and the two unlocked SP merges worked (`s3-local`, `d-local-merge-sections`, `sp-local-unlocked-into-plain`, `sp-local-plain-into-unlocked`). And S1 failed **after** copying two pages. So: a short probe of subpages versus cross-notebook before entry 6 is designed; hrMergeFailed carries `outcomeUnknown`; and E6's result reports the source as untouched and the copies under new IDs. Note "most plain merges fail" overstates it: two of five plain or unlocked merges failed.
5. **A cross-notebook section move can raise a modal dialog** (entry 12). M3 to the throwaway's root blocked 55 s on "hasn't finished downloading", then returned `0x80042004` and left a copy behind (`m3-local-to-root`, `-024952`); the same kind of move into a group took 173 ms (`m3-local-into-group`). So the design needs: a call limit that covers a user answering a dialog, or an explicit `Timeout` with `outcomeUnknown`; after any error, a re-read of both parents by name for a duplicate; and a result that says so.
6. **Restore works by the target section's XML, and the page gets a new ID** (entry 5). R1, R2 same notebook and R2 another notebook all restored, each under a new ID, out of Deleted Pages (`r1-local`, `r2-local-same`, `r2-local-other`, `-024952`); a section returns with its ID (`r3-local`). Entry 5 is feasible: its result must return the new page ID, and the journal can't key on the recycled one. RP (protected) is pending sitting B.
7. **OneNote doesn't guard the recycle bin, so the server must** (entries 5, 12, and 5.4's `update_hierarchy`). R5 found no `0x8004202E`: a recycled section can be renamed, a live section listed under `OneNote_RecycleBin` is soft-deleted, and Deleted Pages can be reordered (`r5-local-*`, `-024952`). AD-7 should refuse any hierarchy write that names a recycle-bin parent, except restore's own route. `UpdatePageContent` on a recycled page gives `0x8004200C`, which AD-9's current `userAction` misdescribes (`r5-local-update-recycled-page`).
8. **Notebook export only as a package** (entry 7). Every other format failed with `0x80042006` or `0x80042014` and wrote nothing (`x1-local-notebook-*`, `-024202`), so entry 7 refuses them before COM, as it already does a section group.
9. **A notebook package keeps protected sections encrypted** (entry 7's E8 rule). XP1 and XP2 found only encrypted section files in the package, unlocked and locked (`xp1-local`, `xp2-local`, `-025556`), unlike a section's `pfOneNote` backup (5.1 constraint 17). So a notebook package with a protected section in it may not need that section's read grant. What opening such a package shows (O5) is pending sitting B, and should be settled before entry 7 relies on this.
10. **`open_package` must open the notebook itself** (entry 8). `OpenPackage` unpacked into the folder it was given and opened no notebook (`o1-local`, `o2-local`, `-024202`; `d-local-open-package`, `-024952`). "The opened notebook in the result" needs an `OpenHierarchy` of the path out, and a wait of up to about 5 s before the pages are listed. The destination needn't be empty: O2 unpacked beside a marker file. A destination that's already an open notebook returns that notebook (O3).
11. **A page move leaves its subpages behind** (entry 4). M1d's child stayed in the source and became a level-1 page (`m1d-local-child`, `-024952`). Entry 4 must move the subpages in the same call, refuse a page that has subpages, or report the orphaned children.
12. **Merges and moves can take protected content out of protection** (entries 4, 6 and E8). With the section unlocked, a merge copied a protected section's pages into a plain section, and a move took a page out, both landing unencrypted (`sp-local-unlocked-into-plain`, `mp-local-page-out-unlocked`, `-025556`). AD-21's read grant should cover any move or merge out of a protected section into an unprotected one. Moving a whole protected section keeps `encrypted` and its ID, but changes its path and so its `SectionKey` (`mp-local-section-unlocked-to-*`), which entry 12 must handle for read grants and journal entries.
13. **Every call tried except `MergeFiles` worked with File > Options open** (`d-local-get-hierarchy`, `d-local-publish`, `d-local-open-package`, `d-local-merge-sections`, `-024952`). So `ExternalWrite` can't rely on `0x80042030` to detect a dialog; it doesn't need to wait for one either. `UpdateHierarchy` under a dialog stays `0x80042030` (5.1 constraint 11); D's move would recheck it and is pending sitting B.
14. **A timed-out export can still produce its file** (entries 3 and 13). X3's file was complete-sized 1 ms after the timeout, and a retry gets `FileExists` (`x3-local-*`, `-030642`). The export's `Timeout` result should name the output path, so the user can check it, and a retry needs a new name or the user's say-so to replace it.
