# Findings: write probes, part 3: exports, move, merge and recycle-bin restore

**Draft, both sittings in.** Sitting A (local, 2026-11-16 UTC, early hours) and sitting B (cloud, the protected reruns, sitting A's leftovers and the label scenario, 2026-11-16 UTC, afternoon and evening) have both run. Every Matrix row now has a result or a stated reason it wasn't probed. The status stays `draft` until the Fable final review and Phill's approval. Nothing here goes into the spine memlog until the doc is final, apart from the two Decisions Phill has already made (story 3.9 dropped; the six sitting-A rules approved for 3.2, memlog lines 286–287).

**Top finding: four calls or sequences end ONENOTE.EXE.** Three are reproduced from sitting A: `Publish(pfOneNote2007)` of a page (twice), `MergeFiles` (three times, once with File > Options open), and `UpdateHierarchy` moving a page out of a **locked** protected section (once). The first two return `0x800706BE` RPC_S_CALL_FAILED after 1.7–3.0 s; the locked move left no row at all. Sitting B added a fourth, seen once: OneNote crashed about 2 s after a local→cloud section move returned ok, while the probe was setting up its next case, and the moved section then never synced (Crashes). The same moves with a 10 s settle after each didn't crash. After one sitting-A crash OneNote didn't relaunch, and on the next two starts it showed its safe-start prompt (observed by Phill). So no tool may send the first three calls, and a cross-storage or cross-notebook section move needs a settle before the next write.

**Short answer:**

1. **Exports.** Page and section publish in PDF, XPS, Word, MHTML, HTML, EMF and OneNotePackage, local and cloud, each writing exactly one file, with near-identical sizes and times on both. A notebook publishes as a package on both; **PDF, XPS and EMF worked for the cloud throwaway** but failed for the local one; Word, MHTML and HTML failed on both. Packages land 250–530 ms after the call returns; the others are on disk at the first poll. Word is the slow one (1.0–3.9 s).
2. **A timed-out export isn't a broken one.** The 30-page Word export passed its 1 s limit, its file was on disk 1 ms later at full size and never grew, and a retry to the same path is `0x8004201A` hrFileAlreadyExists, leaving the file untouched.
3. **A notebook package keeps protected sections encrypted**, locked or unlocked, local and cloud (XP1, XP2, four runs). **Opening such a package shows the section locked, with no password prompt** (O5), even when it was packaged unlocked. That is the opposite of a section's `pfOneNote` backup, which is plaintext (5.1 constraint 17).
4. **`OpenPackage` unpacks but doesn't open.** It wrote the notebook's files straight into the folder it was given and opened no notebook, in all five tries; the probe's own `OpenHierarchy` opened it.
5. **`MergeSections` mostly works, but fails unpredictably.** 15 of 18 plain or unlocked merges worked, subpages included, across notebooks and across storage (local↔cloud). The three `0x8004201F` hrMergeFailed were S1 within MCP Test and S2 to each throwaway notebook; **two of the three had already copied some pages**. It copies (the source is untouched) and the copies get new IDs. A locked source or destination is `0x8004201B`, three runs out of three.
6. **Page moves work everywhere; section moves between notebooks don't always.** A page moves by one call with the target section's XML, gets a new ID and keeps its dates, within and across notebooks and in both storage directions; its **subpage stays behind**, promoted to level 1. A section moved from a cloud notebook into a group of another notebook failed twice with `0x80042004` (cloud throwaway, and local), while all three moves out of a local notebook into another notebook's group worked. Section moves to another notebook's root worked four times out of six; one (local) raised a dialog and left a copy, and one (cloud, sent straight after another move) blocked behind a dialog. A section group moved to the cloud throwaway's root failed with `0x80042018`.
7. **Restore works through `UpdateHierarchy`,** local and cloud. A recycled page listed in any section's XML comes back with a new ID; a recycled section, protected ones too, comes back with its ID. A protected section is locked in the recycle bin and is restored still locked. OneNote never returned `0x8004202E`. `UpdatePageContent` on a recycled page is `0x8004200C` hrPageReadOnly.
8. **A modal dialog blocks only `UpdateHierarchy`.** With File > Options open, `GetHierarchy`, `Publish(pfPDF)`, `OpenPackage` and `MergeSections` worked (twice each), `MergeFiles` crashed, and a page move returned `0x80042030` hrAppInModalUI at once. A dialog OneNote raises itself can instead block a move for as long as it stays open (twice: 55 s until Phill answered it, and 180 s until the probe's call limit).
9. **Protected content can leave protection.** With the section unlocked, merges and page moves carry pages from a protected section into a plain one, where they sit unencrypted (SP, MP, three runs).
10. **Labels: unknown.** The tenant offers no sensitivity labels, so `0x80042031` wasn't probed.

**How it ran.** Opt-in scenarios in `tests/OneNoteMcp.Integration/WriteProbes/` (`Round2ExportProbes.cs`, `Round2MergeProbes.cs`, `Round2MoveProbes.cs`, `Round2RestoreProbes.cs`, `Round2ProtectedProbes.cs`, `Round2TimeoutProbe.cs`, `Round2LabelProbe.cs`), run on 2026-11-16 (UTC) by the main session with Phill present. ## Implications for entry 2 (3.2 spine amendments) and the round-2 stories

Proposals for Phill, not decisions, except where a Decision is named. Phill approved six of them for 3.2 on 2026-11-16 (memlog line 287): 1, 3, 4's `outcomeUnknown`, 7, 8 and 11. Each point cites its rows and says how strong the evidence is: **one run**, or **repeated** (two or more runs or cases).

1. **`publish` never offers `pfOneNote2007`** (entry 13's format list, entry 3's tracer). **Approved for 3.2.** It crashed OneNote both times it was sent (`x1-local-page-pfOneNote2007`, `-023939`, `-024627`; repeated). The format enum leaves it out, and the gateway refuses it before COM in case a caller passes the raw value.
2. **`MergeFiles` crashes OneNote every time,** three out of three (`f1-local`, `-024202`, `-024714`; `d-local-merge-files`, `-024952`; repeated). **Decided: story 3.9 (`merge_files`) is dropped** (memlog line 286); E4 is closed by that Decision and 3.10 follows 3.8. Even a target on disk doesn't mean it worked: D's crash left a 10,104-byte file.
3. **Moves refuse a locked source or destination before any COM call** (entries 4 and 12). **Approved for 3.2** for moves out of a locked section. A page moved out of a locked section crashed OneNote (`local-locked`, `-025556`; one run). A move into a locked section wasn't tried, but a merge into one returned `0x8004201B` (point 4), and refusing costs nothing. The same goes for moving a locked section until a probe shows it's safe (not probed).
4. **`merge_sections` (entry 6): a locked section is refused; hrMergeFailed is `outcomeUnknown`.**
   - **A locked source or destination is `0x8004201B` and changes nothing,** local and cloud (`sp-local-locked-*`, `-025556`, `-195430`; `sp-cloud-locked-*`, `-191000`; repeated, three runs). AD-7 refuses it before COM from `locked`, and maps the code to `SectionLocked` if it slips through.

The cause is unknown (Evidence gaps); a short probe of merges into a throwaway versus another notebook would narrow it, but needn't hold up entry 6.
   - E6's result reports the source as untouched and the copies under new IDs (repeated).
5. **A cross-notebook section or group move can fail, half-succeed or raise a dialog** (entry 12).

Local and cloud: R1, R2 same notebook and R2 another notebook all restored, each under a new ID, out of Deleted Pages (`r1-*`, `r2-*`, `-024952`, `-154342`; repeated); a section returns with its ID (`r3-*`, same runs; repeated). A soft-deleted protected section is locked in the recycle bin even if it was unlocked before, so restore needs no unlock, and no read grant, to put it back.
